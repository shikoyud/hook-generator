How to use the bot

1. Install the bot

  1. Use Chrome, Edge or Brave. Step 2 needs a feature Firefox does not have.
  2. Install the Tampermonkey extension from your browser's web store.
  3. Click farmbot.user.js. Tampermonkey opens an install screen; press Install.
  4. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, open Tampermonkey's Details and turn on Allow User Scripts (older versions: Developer mode). Without this, Tampermonkey cannot run scripts.

2. Put the hook line into the game (no server access needed)

The bot needs one line added to the end of the game's code. You do that with the browser's built-in Override content feature: the browser keeps your edited copy of the file and uses it instead of the original, only on your computer.

  1. Press Generate at the top of this page and copy the line.
  2. Open farmingseason.io in another tab and press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I) to open DevTools.
  3. Click the Sources tab at the top of DevTools. If you do not see it, click the » button to find it.
  4. In the left panel, under Page, expand farmingseason.io, then assets. Click the file whose name starts with game- and ends with .js (there is also a game-….css next to it: that is the wrong one). It should be the same name this page showed after Generate.
  5. Right-click on the file's content and choose Override content.
  6. The first time, Chrome asks for a folder to keep overrides in: create a new empty folder anywhere (for example Documents\fs-override), select it, then click Allow on the yellow bar that appears at the top of DevTools.
  7. The file is now editable. Click inside it, press Ctrl+End to jump to the very end, press Enter to start a new last line, and paste the hook line. Press Ctrl+S to save. A small purple dot on the file icon means the override is active.
  8. Reload the game with F5, keeping DevTools open. The bot panel appears at the bottom right and its log says Ready. Hook found.

Important: overrides only work while DevTools is open. If you close DevTools, the game loads the original file and the bot reports that the hook is missing. You can drag DevTools to a separate window or make it small, but keep it open while the bot runs.

After a game update the file gets a new name and your old override no longer matches. Press Generate again and repeat these steps with the new file. The bot tells you when this happens.

3. Run it

  1. Sign in and open your farm as usual.
  2. In the Planner tab of the bot panel, choose what each field should grow. The dropdown shows grow time, value and value per hour. Use the Fill all buttons for a quick start, or leave fields on Auto to replant whatever was there last.
  3. In the Bot tab press Start farming. Leave the tab open and visible.

On its own, each round, the farming side: feeds hungry animals (closest to starving first), collects products, harvests ready fields, picks ripe orchard fruit, pumps the oil well once a day, empties the mine when its two carts are full, plants empty fields from your plan, and collects village income. It never goes to the arena by itself.

Battle is a separate button. Press it when you want to fight: the bot walks to the arena, starts a run, moves, dodges and picks perks, and keeps starting new runs until energy drops under 5, the daily loot cap is full, or you press Stop battle. Farming pauses while battle mode runs and resumes afterwards. A run costs 5 energy; the panel shows how much energy today's animal meals still need, so you can decide for yourself.

Toggles in the Bot tab:

  • keep energy for meals (off by default): when on, planting waits while energy minus 2 would drop under what today's remaining animal meals need: 2 per animal building for each meal still to come (two meals before noon, one after). Six buildings in the afternoon is 6 × 1 × 2 = 12. The status shows that arithmetic. Off, the bot plants whenever it has 2 energy; hungry animals are still fed first.
  • wait up to 5 min to harvest together (on by default): if a field is ready but others will ripen within five minutes, the bot waits and harvests them all in one go for 2 energy. Feeding and collecting carry on meanwhile.
  • batch jobs (on by default): when two or more fields are ready or empty, the bot uses the game's own Harvest all / Plant all, which costs 2 energy for the whole batch instead of 2 per field, and each field still gets its own crop. Hungry animals are fed in one burst and products collected in one burst too. Note the game charges feeding 2 per building per meal: eight cows in two four-cow barns cost 4 per meal no matter how they are fed.
  • feed first (Planner tab, on by default): the bot reads how much your animals eat per day and how many bags and grain are in the silo. While stock covers less than the days you set, every empty field grows wheat or corn, whichever is shorter, counting fields already growing grain. Once covered, the field gets the crop you chose. Grain is mixed into bags automatically. So with 320 bags a day the bot keeps roughly three wheat and three corn fields busy and gives the rest to your best crop.
  • auto-buy energy (off by default): buys only when the work right in front of the bot cannot be paid for (this meal, the waiting harvests, the fields to plant), never just because energy is low. A checkbox for each reason it may buy for: meals (today's remaining animal meals), waiting harvests (ready crops, ripe fruit, products, a full mine, today's oil), extra crop cycles (only while they fit under today's harvest cap, as many as the day has time for, and only if a cycle earns at least 1.5× its energy cost; silo crops never count), and battle runs (between arena runs, 5 more while the loot cap is open and the last run paid at least 1.5× the price). Buys just what is short, rounded up to 10, at most 300 a day in total, never more than half the wallet; an optional "buy ahead" box makes buys bigger and rarer instead. Bought energy never expires in the game.
  • village income: collect town-hall income automatically (every 30 minutes once at least 1 token has accrued).
  • cap-aware planting: crops, fruit and animal products share one daily harvest cap. With this on, the bot plants a cash crop only if its value still fits under today's cap, counting what animals and the orchard will already bring. Once the cap is reached it plants wheat or corn for the silo instead. Silo crops are never capped.

4. Good to know

  • Energy. Every job costs 2 energy and a battle run 5. The farming side keeps enough energy for today's remaining animal meals before planting. Battle mode spends whatever you let it: watch the "meals still need" figure in the panel.
  • The human check. The game sometimes shows a "are you a real farmer" box. The bot pauses the moment it appears and never touches it. Tick the box yourself; the bot resumes by itself.
  • Pace. The bot does roughly one job every 3 to 8 seconds with random pauses, well inside the game's limits, and shows its current jobs per minute in the Bot tab. It is not meant to be fast.
  • Battle. Keep the browser tab visible during a fight: the game pauses runs in hidden tabs. The bot moves, dodges and picks perks for you: it sidesteps arrows and fireballs before they arrive and dashes only at the last moment, walks out of a bomber's blast, steps off a bat's lunge line, kites when its health is low, dodges the Rat King's mortar strikes, and picks perks in a sensible order (damage reduction and dodge first). A run retreats at wave 30 and a new one starts right away while energy and the loot cap allow. Stop battle retreats from the current run first, then leaves the arena.
  • Stopping. Stop farming and Stop battle are independent. Closing the tab stops both. Settings and your crop plan are kept in the browser for next time.
  • Help. If something goes wrong, the panel shows a Help button that opens this page.

Troubleshooting

No panel at allTampermonkey is off for this site, "Allow User Scripts" is not enabled, or the script was not installed.
"window.__fs not found"The hook line is not in the file the game loaded. Usually DevTools was closed. Open it, check the purple dot on the file, reload.
"these entries point at the wrong thing"The game updated after you made the override. Press Generate, then redo step 2 with the new file.
Status "holding: rate" or "holding: regular"Normal. The bot is spacing its jobs to stay under the game's limits.
Status "paused: human check"Solve the check box in the game. The bot continues on its own.
